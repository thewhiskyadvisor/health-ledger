Privacy Policy

Health Ledger is a personal, single-user health tracking tool built and used solely by its owner. It is not distributed to or used by any other individuals.

The app connects to the Oura API to retrieve the owner's own daily sleep, readiness, activity, and related wellness data. This data is written to a private Google Sheet owned and controlled by the same individual, and displayed back within the app for personal reference.

No data is sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party. No advertising or analytics services are used.
